Statistical Modelling course

Third-year course of the B.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence (Universities of Milano-Bicocca, Milano and Pavia).
A.A. 2026/2027, 1st semester.


Communications
  • Sign up for the course on the Kiro platform to receive communications via email.
  • Office hours (online or in-person): send me an email to schedule a mStataleeeting.
  • Exam Instructions:
    • The exam lasts two hours and consists of exercises similar to those covered during the course.
    • In addition to the exercises, there may be one or two open questions requiring a more interpretative and/or theoretical answer.
    • You may bring a cheat sheet to the exam: one single-sided A4 sheet on which you may write anything you find useful before the exam and which you may consult during the exam.
    • Apart from the cheat sheet, the exam is closed book: you are not allowed to consult any other materials during the exam. The use of mobile phones or smartwatches is strictly prohibited. Any violation will be dealt with in accordance with the University regulations.
    • You may (and are strongly encouraged to) bring a non-programmable scientific calculator.
